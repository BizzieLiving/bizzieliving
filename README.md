# Hi, I'm Stephanie Elie 👋🏾

**Staff Content Designer · AI Product Content · Audio Drama Creator · Founder**

I'm a writer and a tech nerd — and I've never been willing to choose between the two. By day, I design content systems and conversational AI experiences for product teams. By night (and weekends, and early mornings), I build worlds through audio drama and run [Bizzie Media Group](https://bizziemediagroup.com), an independent production company focused on Black stories in audio and emerging formats.

I believe the best experiences — in product and in storytelling — come from the same place: clarity, intention, and knowing exactly who you're building for.

---

## 🧭 Core Values

**Build because the need is clear.**
I don't wait for permission to solve a problem. Whether it's a content governance gap, a broken onboarding experience, or a story that hasn't been told yet — I identify what's missing and make it.

**Representation is non-negotiable.**
The specificity of a story is the story. I protect the integrity of the communities I write for, especially in spaces where those voices are underrepresented.

**Systems over heroics.**
One good piece of copy is a win. A scalable content system that enables a whole team is a legacy. I build for leverage.

**Credentials speak. I don't need to shout.**
The work stands on its own. I prefer plainspoken honesty over polished posturing — in writing and in life.

**Creative and technical aren't opposites.**
My screenwriting background informs how I build frameworks. My systems thinking shapes how I construct narrative. Both sides of my brain stay on.
